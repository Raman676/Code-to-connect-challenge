LIMITATIONS
====================================================================================================
- The main label uses the same anchor as the sentiment labels, so |r1| includes the news day: text that describes the move ('shares trading lower', 'halted') can 'predict' its size. See the NEXT-day comparison.
- Same-day counts are reported only as a comparison: they include items published after the item and are partly caused by the move itself.
- Tweet counts exist only on 121 dates (2017-2018); elsewhere the flag is 0 and the count carries no information.
- Ridge predictions are pulled towards the average, so predicted severity on the training cut-points rarely reaches 1 or 10; the 'decile of predictions' table shows the ranking quality instead.
- News type (to be added) is not yet a feature.
