LIMITATIONS
====================================================================================================
- Labels come from the market reaction, not from reading the text: every item about a company on one day shares that day's label, so the text often carries little information about it.
- Tweets have no gap between splits: a story spanning a cut-off (e.g. Nike's Kaepernick ad, early Sept 2018) can appear in train and validation.
- The tweet test period (27 Sep - 30 Oct 2018) was a market sell-off: impact 1 is far more common than in training, so results there partly measure a regime change.
- Product names (iPhone, Windows, Prime, Air Jordan, Xbox games ...) are not masked and still identify companies; masking of names/tickers is rule-based and can miss variants or misspellings.
- All @mentions are masked as [COMPANY], including mentions of ordinary users.
- The 10-day news gap uses business days (exchange holidays not removed).
- FinBERT is used frozen (no fine-tuning); the PhraseBank has a neutral class that our 6-class model cannot predict, so neutral sentences are only described.
